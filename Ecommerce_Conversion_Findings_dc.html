APPENDIX — METHODOLOGY

How this analysis was built

Data
Full site clickstream, 2 months, ~110M events (views, cart adds, purchases).
Approach
A machine-learning tool that scores each cart by how the shopper was behaving in that visit. We tried scoring by "what they browsed" first, but it wasn't reliable enough on its own — "how they browsed" is what actually works.
Validation
Patterns identified on October, confirmed out-of-sample on November — including Black Friday week.
